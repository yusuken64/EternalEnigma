Balance:
TurtleShell: death explosion, 20 physical damage to living units within one
Chebyshev tile with an open step between them. Hits either team; active Silence
suppresses damage and the fire burst. Each death resolves once.
Explosion collateral grants no EXP to the dead turtle.
MetalSlime: spawn weight 1 versus normal weight 20 on its existing floor range.
25% warp chance on a positive, nonlethal combat hit (no miss or environmental
damage). Uses a different reachable, unoccupied cell fitting its footprint.
Werewolf: packs of up to 3 at floor generation; neighbors must be walkable,
unoccupied and not reserved for another generated enemy. Crowding reduces size.
Nessie means the worm emerging from the ground (user clarification):
WormMonster receives the root attack; Skeleton loses it.

Class assignments are fixed on prefabs; existing protagonist class override
semantics are retained. Secondary classes must differ from primary, and each
primary/secondary pair is unique.
