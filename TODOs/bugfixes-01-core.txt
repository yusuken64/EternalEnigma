Implementation and verification (2026-10-05):
- Dungeon entry carries the town balance; return replaces the balance once.
  Defeat and retreat retain the existing configured loot policy.
- Recruit weapon models now reflect their actual equipment, removing prefab
  weapon duplicates. Recruitment and reload preserve one equipped item and
  do not copy the leader's equipment into the recruit or shared bag.
- Each campaign dungeon grants its own named, permanent party passive.
  Completed-dungeon records restore these abilities, including older clears
  and later recruits. Re-clears do not stack rewards; respec retains them.
- Equipment no longer depends on a consumable effect definition. Class
  proficiency restrictions are checked and explained in equipment menus.
- Four-person town walking previously waited 3 extra frames after arrival;
  a shared tween wait reduces this to at most 1 frame in the regression test.
  Warm four-member sight/minimap updates: 0.967 ms before, 0.149-0.260 ms
  after (100 updates, same seeded entry floor, Unity Editor). This measures
  that update path, not whole-frame performance on every device/floor.
- Save cards retain initial focus, support left/right and Back navigation,
  activate on Enter, and visibly distinguish the selected slot.
