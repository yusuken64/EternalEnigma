Priority 3: Economy and persistent resources

Adopted behavior (implemented 2026-10-05)

- Hunger and its partial accumulation persist through clears/retreats, town
  travel, active-party changes and saves. HasHunger distinguishes old saves
  from saved zero hunger. Free inn/home rest restores HP/SP/hunger and resets
  accumulation. Only dungeon turns advance hunger.
- Campaign defeat restores checkpoint stock, vitals and run history.
  Standalone defeat retains full recovery and does not replenish shops.
- Shop offers use the town's campaign Tier (otherwise Tier 0). Display,
  purchase and replenishment share one resolver without mutating shared assets.
- RestockCycle and ReturnCommitted remain authoritative. Each clear or retreat
  advances the global cycle once, including repeat clears. All shops apply the
  new cycle on their next access. First access initializes stock; menus, rest,
  transactions, travel, progression and saving/loading never replenish it.
  Depleted saved stock is preserved; new offers wait for the next return.
- Return audit: real campaign first clear, repeat clear and retreat replenish
  correctly in regression tests. The original failure to replenish was not
  reproduced. The confirmed defect was that defeat/abandon also advanced the
  cycle; those paths now leave it unchanged.
- Buy/Sell modes require confirmation. Selling removes the exact bag entry
  with its remaining quantity. TownServices rejects equipped, progression,
  depleted and already-sold entries. Cancellation and duplicate confirmation
  cannot pay twice. Sold goods never enter stock. Material prices are unchanged.
  Ordinary sale value is floor(lowest catalog price / offered quantity *
  remaining quantity / 4). Uncatalogued unit values default to 100G for
  consumables or max(100, 50 * (Strength + Defense)) for equipment.
- Existing equipment offers remain. Other existing equipment is added once
  at Tiers 1/2/3/4 when max(Strength, Defense) <= 6/9/12/20, one each, priced
  at max(100, 50 * (Strength + Defense)). The 20HP/125G potion remains.
  Tiers 1-4 add 40/60/80/100HP potions for 250/375/500/625G, four each.
- Bakeries stock six pastries and three of each drink, retaining earlier tiers:
  T0 Bread: 50 hunger / 100G.
  T1 Slime Jelly Danish: 75 hunger / 150G.
  T2 Smiling Mushroom & Cheese Croissant: 100 hunger / 200G.
  T3 Crab Monster Meat Croquette: 125 hunger / 250G.
  T4 Dragon Sausage & Onion Pastry: 150 hunger / 300G.
  T0 Cactus Pulp Caramel Coffee / Cactus Pulp Caramel Cloud Latte.
  T1 Roasted Battle Bee Espresso / Battle Bee Butter Honey Latte.
  T2 Salamander-Fat Caramel Cold Brew / Salamander-Fat Caramel Latte.
  T3 Specter Cream Shakerato / Specter Cream Tiramisu Latte.
  T4 Beholder Eye Jelly Double Espresso / Beholder Eye Cream Pistachio Latte.
  Coffee SP: 5/10/15/20/25; price: 150/300/450/600/750G.
  Latte HP: 20/40/60/80/100; SP: 5/10/15/20/25;
  price: 250/500/750/1000/1250G. Descriptions identify actual monster
  ingredients; pastry and drink monsters are separate. Recovery caps apply.
- Specialties are persistent shop-only assets. Restoration searches catalogs
  without expanding loot eligibility. ItemManager.DungeonLoot filters ShopOnly.
  Enemy drops (MovementAction), floor items (Game), and chests/props (DungeonProp)
  all use that pool. Campaign rewards grant keys, capabilities and abilities,
  not bakery items. Normal Bread stays in Common's loot list. Purchased
  specialties restore by unique name and work in dungeons. Bread/potion visuals
  and existing use actions remain; no new art, crafting, harvesting or sizes.
