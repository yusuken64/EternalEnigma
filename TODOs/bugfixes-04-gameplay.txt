Priority 4: Skills, classes, and enemy behavior

1. Separate the skill menu from inventory.
2. Selecting an active skill should enter the casting/targeting
   flow directly, without an extra cast confirmation.
3. Match hero primary classes to their appearance.
4. Distinguish heroes sharing a primary class with secondary classes.
5. Make spike turtles explode on death.
   Silence must prevent the explosion.
6. Make metal slimes rare spawns with a chance to warp when hit.
7. Give rat assassins unlimited ranged attacks.
8. Move the root ability from skeletons to Nessie.
9. Make wolves spawn in packs.

Verification:
Check skill targeting/cancellation, class equipment compatibility,
silenced deaths, warp placement, and enemy spawn/attack behavior.
Choose concrete class mappings and balance values before implementation.
