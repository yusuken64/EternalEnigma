# Hero story text

Draft opening and ending blurbs for the 24 selectable protagonists. These are narrative copy only; no gameplay changes are implied. Hero IDs distinguish duplicate names. Existing prefab descriptions sometimes describe skills that differ from selectable combat classes; the copy treats those as background, not gameplay promises.

## MC01 — Avery
**Start:** Avery has studied magic since childhood, but books can only teach so much. The dungeons offer a chance to discover wonders firsthand, alongside companions brave enough to explore them.

**End:** Avery returns with stories and discoveries no book could have supplied. The dungeons have deepened a lifelong love of magic, and the journey has given Avery friends to share it with.

## MC02 — Morgan
**Start:** Morgan grew up admiring martial skill and dreams of becoming a champion in their own right. With a party at their side, they set out to face the strongest challenges the dungeons can offer.

**End:** Morgan has overcome the dungeons' greatest challenges and earned a story worth telling back home. The victories belong to the whole party, and Morgan wears that achievement proudly.

## MC03 — Rowan
