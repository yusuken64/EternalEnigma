5. Scout navigation and overworld directions
------------------------------------------
[x] Add an ability that directs an allied Scout along a path straight to an exit.
[x] Make overworld signs interactable and provide directions to nearby landmarks.

Implemented: Find Exit is a tier-1, one-rank Scout skill costing 2 SP. It guides
the caster (controlled or follower) to undiscovered stairs via normal turns,
stops for danger/blocked routes/manual interruption and never exits the floor.
Signs open road directions/distances to nearby towns and other landmarks.
Controlled and follower navigation, interruption, full-control turns and sign
interaction checks pass. The sign dialog was visually inspected.

Original audit:
- Scout's existing Pathfinder skill increases HungerMax; it does not navigate
  to exits. Floor Sense already reveals layout/stairs, which may support this.
  Define whether the new ability moves the currently controlled ally or an AI
  follower, and whether it can target an undiscovered exit.
- BiomeRoadSigns already plans directions/distances to towns and renders town
  labels. Interaction and directions to other nearby landmarks remain.

Starting points: Assets/Resources/Classes/Skills/Scout/Pathfinder.asset;
Assets/Scripts/Dungeon/Actions/RevealActions.cs;
Assets/Scripts/Environment/BiomeRoadSigns.cs;
Assets/Scripts/Overworld/OverworldScene.cs.
